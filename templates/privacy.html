Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and login credentials when you register an account. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions of your transactions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service — creating and securing your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating spending summaries and reports. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and are accessible only to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party services for functionality such as hosting, fonts, and infrastructure. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. We're happy to help with any concerns about your data or privacy.
